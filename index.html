WristLens

See your phone camera's picture on your Amazfit watch, and take the photo from your wrist.

Free beta · 15-day trial
Download the phone app (Android) How to install

What it does

Your camera app, on your wrist

Works with the camera app already on your phone: same lenses, modes and photo quality.

Frame the shot

Prop your phone up, step into the picture, and check the framing live on your watch.

Take the photo

Press your watch's button or tap the shutter on screen. The phone takes the photo.

Private

The picture goes straight from your phone to your watch. It never reaches any server.

What you need

An Android phone

Android 10 or newer, with the Zepp app.

A Zepp OS watch

Amazfit Bip 6 tested. Other Zepp OS 4.2+ watches, square or round, are supported in the beta.

It shows a small, low-frame-rate preview (about 1–2 frames a second): enough to frame a shot, not to watch video.

Install

  1. Phone app: download the APK above and open it. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: allow it.
  2. Watch app: install WristLens on your watch from the Zepp app. (Store listing coming soon.)
  3. Open WristLens on the phone and follow the 4 setup steps: sign in with your email, enter the code your watch shows, mark the camera area from a screenshot, and turn on the shutter helper.
  4. Shutter helper says "Restricted setting"? Settings → Apps → WristLens → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again. Android does this for apps not from the Play Store.
  5. Tap Start preview, choose "Entire screen", open your camera app, then open WristLens on your watch.

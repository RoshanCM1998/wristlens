Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

The camera picture

The phone app captures your screen only while you have started the preview, shrinks it, and sends it over Bluetooth to WristLens on your watch through the Zepp app. The picture never leaves your phone and watch: it is not uploaded, stored or seen by us.

The shutter helper (an Android accessibility service) looks at your camera app's on-screen buttons to find the shutter, and presses it when your watch asks for a photo. What it sees stays on your phone; it is switched on only while the preview runs (when allowed).

What we store

  • Your email address — to sign you in with a one-time code and to contact you about your licence.
  • A scrambled ID of your watch (a one-way hash, not the real ID) — so each watch gets one free trial and a licence belongs to one watch.
  • Your phone's Android ID for this app — so each phone gets one free trial.
  • Trial and licence dates, and whether you have bought a licence.

We don't use analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

Where it is kept

On Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database). Sign-in emails are sent from wristlens.app@gmail.com through Google. Payments, once available, will be handled by Razorpay or Dodo Payments; we never see your card details.

Your choices

Email wristlens.app@gmail.com to see or delete your data. Deleting it ends any trial or licence linked to it.
